Find what the user is after in their Magnific history: $ARGUMENTS

- Use `creations_search` to look, and `creations_show` to pull up a specific asset.
- Summarize matches in a compact list — what it is, when, and the link — rather than
  dumping raw tool output.
- Offer the obvious next step: use a past asset as the starting point for a new
  generation, upscale it, or cut out its background. Reusing beats regenerating —
  it costs nothing to find and keeps the look consistent.
- If nothing matches, say so and ask for a different description before generating
  something new.
